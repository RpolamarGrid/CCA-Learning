Acme Corp Refund and Account Policy — Internal Reference

Section 1: Identity Verification
All refund requests must undergo account ownership verification before any
refund is issued. Verification requires matching the requester's provided
order ID against the account email on file. Support agents have historically
skipped this step under time pressure, which has led to refunds being issued
to the wrong customer in approximately 8% of cases reviewed in Q3.

[PAGE 2]
Section 2: Refund Thresholds
Refunds under $500 may be processed directly by a support agent once identity
is verified. Refunds of $500 or more require manager sign-off in addition to
identity verification. This threshold exists because high-value refunds carry
disproportionate fraud risk and financial exposure if issued incorrectly.

[PAGE 3]
Section 3: Escalation to Human Review
When a case is escalated to a human agent, the human agent does NOT have
access to the customer's chat transcript. The escalation handoff must
therefore include: customer ID, a summary of the conversation, root cause
analysis, the refund amount if applicable, and a recommended action. Handoffs
that merely reference "see above" are considered non-compliant with this
policy, since the human reviewer cannot see any prior conversation.
